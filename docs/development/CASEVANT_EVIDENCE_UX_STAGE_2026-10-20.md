3. **Materials library:** every case gets the same entry point, including empty/custom/legacy cases. Inventory available exact Canopy versions, original converted correspondence, missing-document disclosures, current working evidence text and recorded URL references. Show provenance and body availability. Download individual available text files and one ZIP with a version/hash manifest and missing-source index. Keep existing reference exports available under details. Export requires existing copy/report authority and exact source/account fences.

5. **Deletion safety:** replace silent/native-confirm node removal with a clear choice to transfer connected evidence in one operation or keep its records in the library. Evidence records are retained when their connections are removed. Cancellation/stale context writes nothing. Regular node deletion removes its other relations only after confirmation; undo restores the complete prior graph. Never delete saved cases or synthetic QA cases.
6. **Node choices and visual system:** display N01/N02… labels and order every node dropdown by the same map index, including filtered/pinned choices.
