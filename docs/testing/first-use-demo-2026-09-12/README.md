## Recording and editorial direction

Use actual product captures with readable labels and a calm pace. A restrained highlight may guide attention; retain enough surrounding UI to show where the action belongs. Preserve the product's paper, ink and brass styling. Show outcomes early and avoid long tours of settings. Technical diagnostics belong only in an explicitly labelled developer-view insert.

Authentication must be performed by the authorized user. Pause capture for private input and label the cut. Do not expose passwords, tokens, email addresses or account identifiers. Avoid inserting a simulated registration-success screen. An unavailable AI service should be shown honestly through the local/manual route, with the corresponding spoken wording adjusted; never animate a fabricated AI answer.

Use `buildCanopyPackage("base")` from `app/canopy-fixture.ts`. The exact identity and source fingerprints are in `segments.json`. D03 v1 distinguishes 300 signed commitments from 600 indicated packs/week; D04 capacity is 480. The declared setup cost of 240,000 and annual contribution of 120,000 imply two-year simple payback. These are synthetic assumptions, not operating results or tax-engine output. Base permits only the defined conditional transition branch; it does not authorize production.

Keep the fictional-data disclosure visible. Do not present an expected branch card as a completed run, a local PDF as a governed output, or a downloaded draft as an independent approval. The source-bound workflow and its reviewer conditions remain intact.
