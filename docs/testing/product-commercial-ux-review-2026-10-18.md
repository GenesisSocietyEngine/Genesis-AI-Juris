## Review method and limits

Source review covers entry, first-case guidance, library metadata, Overview/record navigation, Sources provenance, Decision/palette/inspector, compiler/player access, report options, FAQ/profile and current audit/backlog. Fresh Chrome checks inspect the live entry/library and representative ERP journey; prior v145 receipts supply dated save/drag/export evidence. Commercial findings are product judgments and hypotheses, not market research, customer interviews, willingness-to-pay measurements, legal/security certification or financial projections. ## Product Owner assessment

Strength: category-led examples, independent copies, exact provenance, qualified reports and confirmed save receipts form a coherent professional workflow. Preservation and adverse-state controls have stronger evidence than product breadth. Weakness: a long historical continuation document still mixes completed actions with pending priorities; a current ordered execution contract is needed. Large cases expose a costly detour: search is in Overview, while Decision's List renders all records and the map itself lacks text retrieval. More authoring controls before retrieval/old-client coverage would increase complexity and risk.

Decision: ship a bounded retrieval/onboarding slice, preceded by measured bundle headroom; keep package/account/import/source evidence in the priority backlog. Source-content and governed migration work remain separately versioned. Do not reinterpret synthetic packet completeness as evidence quality.

## Commercial Director assessment

The credible proposition is inspectable decision preparation: sources, options and reasoning remain connected and reopenable. Strong demo proof exists; commercial repeat use, buying authority, willingness to pay, support economics and enterprise readiness are unmeasured. Nine categories describe scope, not nine domain-certified solutions. Current UI cautions against privileged/client-identifiable data, so acquisition and pilots must respect that boundary rather than imply unrestricted client deployment.

Risks: generic entry copy does not quickly name a concrete output; FAQ's blanket simulation description conflicts with professional positioning; deep technical explanations may overwhelm evaluators. A pricing/paywall or invented ROI claim would be premature. Prioritize a repeatable de-identified pilot, buyer/user interviews, service-readiness evidence and a consented activation funnel. Do not instrument raw case text or introduce prices/subscriptions in this slice. Existing application access controls are not end-to-end encryption; no new guarantee is inferred.

## UX assessment

The v145 sticky palette fixes creation proximity and preserves map space. Remaining friction: first-use method shows six steps (Brief/Review/Evidence/Decision map/Test/Report), while the actual user workspace exposes five sections. Optional Training should not define the professional journey. A65-node GreenFire map and larger ERP case need local find/focus as well as drag/zoom. Overview already has full-text search with explicit authored-record status, groups, empty/reset and progressive results; reuse it from Decision rather than create competing semantics.

Accessibility/continuity priorities: keyboard-first retrieval and exact focus, long-text wrapping, actual narrow/short-height measurements, visible loading/retry, no automatic page reload after a deferred panel failure.
