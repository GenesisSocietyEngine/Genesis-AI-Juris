Is this within the committee mandate?
D01 v1 § Mandate: The fictional Investment and Operating Committee has ten days from dossier opening to
choose a controlled managed-site opportunity. This mandate covers premium crops and a 90-day transition
pilot, not commodity-volume expansion.


Do mandatory safety and traceability clearances permit proceeding?
D01 v1 § Safety: A failed or unavailable mandatory independent food-safety or traceability clearance at the
production release gate means no-go regardless of financial attractiveness. No production release is permitted
under the conditional pilot until all release conditions are independently accepted. Clearance state:
no_critical_failure_identified. D06 v1 § Clearance: Base declaration: no critical safety failure is identified. Hard-
stop stress declaration: mandatory independent food-safety or traceability clearance fails or is unavailable at the
production release gate. That declaration requires no-go even with upside economics.


Is sufficient demand signed, not merely indicated?
D03 v1 § Demand: Total indicated demand is 600 packs per week: 300 signed, 180 non-binding LOI and 120
expressions of interest. Only 300 count as signed commitments against the 450 minimum.
