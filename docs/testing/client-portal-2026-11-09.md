# Client portal — 9 November 2026

The acceptance target is an independent client sign-in that exposes only deliberately shared items for one client and one case. Staff keep their selected case when returning to Overview, Calendar, Library or AI Studio. The reference is the [MyCase Client Portal Overview](https://supportcenter.mycase.com/en/articles/9369919-mycase-client-portal-overview); CaseVant uses its existing Outlook-style workspace and pilot data policy.

Only a case owner can invite or revoke; an active owner or contributor can share individual items and exchange messages. An invitation lasts seven days and requires both the private link and the invited address signed in through ChatGPT. Staff deliver the link themselves. Portal access does not enroll the client in the firm or the case team. A revoked or expired invitation must not unlock earlier history through a new grant.

A document share pins its exact governed file version. A shared event exposes only its reviewed title and time, and a change to these fields hides it until shared again. Internal event notes, evidence metadata, staff identities and other clients are excluded. Each client has an isolated chronological conversation. Uploads remain separate from evidence and require an authenticated download with a matching stored checksum. This pilot accepts PDF, plain text, JPEG and PNG up to 5 MiB per file and 100 MiB or 100 files per recipient grant. File checks are format and integrity checks, not malware scanning.

No client email, real case or real client document is needed for acceptance. No payment, signature, automatic client email or external notification is part of this release.

Both public origins returned the expected runtime identity and every one of 154 current and 70 retained browser assets. The public audience, environment revision 42 (13 entries), D1 binding and R2 bucket were preserved. The [scheduler compatibility check](https://github.com/GenesisSocietyEngine/Genesis-AI-Juris/actions/runs/42602199587) ran in check-only mode and sent no email.

The public portal rendered its sign-in surface on both origins. Anonymous requests for the staff register, client data and a file returned 401 with private, no-store responses. A signed-in provider session opened the client portal, saw no implicit shares, was denied an unavailable case, and returned to its available-case list. Authenticated lookup of nonexistent document and upload shares failed privately.

The designated synthetic production smoke case received one small text fixture and one non-deliverable test invitation. The client register started empty. Staff shared only version 1, downloaded identical bytes through the portal, and confirmed the signed-in non-recipient could not download the same share. Withdrawal disabled the old authorized download URL. The test invitation was revoked without sending or redeeming it. Staff still saw the withdrawn share and revoked grant. Overview, Calendar and Client portal retained the same case identifier. The synthetic original and staff history remain in that smoke case.

The cross-account client upload, message exchange, retry and mid-transfer revocation checks above ran in isolated D1/R2 and browser fixtures. First-time hosted sign-in continuation and a real external client exchange were not exercised in production. The pilot still accepts synthetic or de-identified material only.
