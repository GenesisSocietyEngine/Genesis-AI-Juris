## Discovery questions

1. Which recurring decision would benefit most from a shared record of evidence, assumptions, alternatives and approvals?
2. Who owns that decision, and who must independently review it?
3. Which two or three documents usually control the decision or delay it?
4. What observable improvement would make a small pilot worthwhile?
5. Could a future facilitated session use a sanitized historical example of that decision family?

## Signature Crop storyboard only

A fictional chef requests a 500-pack premium crop concept; the synthetic procurement note commits only 300. Show the contradiction, request clarification, compare a bounded trial with deferral, and identify review/exit conditions. No implemented workflow, customer, financial forecast or cold-test result is claimed.

## Proposed six-week pilot boundary

Use three to five synthetic or explicitly sanitized decisions in one recurring family, with an owner and reviewer. Start with facilitated sessions and no integrations or confidential documents. Measure decision cycle time, clarification loops, evidence gaps identified, report preparation time, reviewer trust and reuse. Establish the baseline and acceptance thresholds jointly before starting; these are proposed measures, not achieved results.
